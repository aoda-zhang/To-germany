# Behavioral Interview Question Bank — Frontend-Focused Full-Stack Engineer

> **Purpose:** A CV-specific practice bank for senior frontend and frontend-heavy full-stack roles in Germany.
>
> **Reference approach:** The categories reflect themes in GreatFrontEnd's Behavioral Interview Playbook and the behavioral-interview guidance in the Front End Interview Handbook: concrete past examples, individual contribution, collaboration, judgement, self-awareness, measurable outcomes, and reflection. These are original practice prompts tailored to Aoda's background, not copied GreatFrontEnd questions.
>
> **How to use:** Each category contains at least 20 prompts. Do not prepare 200 separate scripts. Build a small set of truthful STAR stories, then practise adapting the emphasis to the question. Use “I” to explain your own actions and “we” for shared outcomes. Only claim technical details, conflict, mistakes, stakeholder reactions, or metrics you can verify.

## Final practice checklist

- [ ] Prepare a 90–120 second self-introduction aligned with the target role.
- [ ] Build five to seven truthful STAR stories and practise adapting them across categories.
- [ ] Prepare at least one real example each for conflict, mistake/failure, feedback, and changed priorities.
- [ ] Verify every metric against the final CV and be ready to explain its definition and source.
- [ ] For each story, distinguish personal actions from team outcomes.
- [ ] Prepare a meaningful reflection: what you learned and what you would change.
- [ ] Practise follow-up questions; interviewers may probe decisions, alternatives, ownership, evidence, and results.
- [ ] Prepare a company-specific “Why us?” answer for every interview.
- [ ] Do not invent incidents or implementation details to fit a question.
- [ ] Keep spoken answers concise, natural, and in language you can comfortably deliver under interview pressure.
