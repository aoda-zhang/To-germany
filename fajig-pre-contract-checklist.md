# FAJIG: Pre-Contract Questions and Due-Diligence Checklist

Use this checklist before signing a contract or paying FAJIG (Find a Job in Germany). The goal is to understand exactly what is included, what FAJIG expects me to do myself, what happens if the job search takes longer than expected, and what financial obligations the contract creates.

**Important:** Treat verbal promises as unconfirmed until they are reflected in the contract or confirmed in writing by an authorized representative. This checklist is for due diligence, not legal advice.

## 1. Service scope: what exactly am I buying?

Ask:

- [ ] Could you walk me through every service included in my package, from onboarding until the service ends?
- [ ] Which deliverables will I receive, and in what format? Could you provide a written list?
- [ ] Who will be my primary coach or contact person? What are their relevant experience and qualifications?
- [ ] Is the service individualized to my profile as a frontend-focused full-stack engineer with 9+ years of experience, or is it mainly a standard curriculum?
- [ ] How many one-to-one sessions are included, how long is each session, and what is the booking/cancellation policy?
- [ ] Are there any additional charges for extra sessions, CV reviews, mock interviews, or other services?
- [ ] What support is included between scheduled sessions (for example, email, chat, or document feedback), and what is the expected response time?
- [ ] What is explicitly **not** included in the package?

**Ask them to confirm in writing:** the service list, deliverables, session count and duration, contact channel, expected response times, and any exclusions or additional fees.

## 2. CV and LinkedIn optimization

Ask:

- [ ] How soon after onboarding will the first CV review be completed?
- [ ] How many revision rounds are included?
- [ ] Will you rewrite/restructure my CV, or provide feedback for me to implement?
- [ ] Will the CV be tailored to German hiring practices and the roles I am targeting (frontend engineer / frontend-focused full-stack engineer)?
- [ ] Do you review my LinkedIn profile, project descriptions, and cover-letter template as well?
- [ ] Will you help align my CV with German job descriptions and applicant tracking systems (ATS) without keyword stuffing or exaggerating my experience?
- [ ] What happens if I am not satisfied with the first version? Is another review included?
- [ ] Can you share an example of the kind of deliverable I should expect, with personal information removed?

**Record the agreed timeline:** onboarding date: ______; first CV feedback due: ______; final agreed version target: ______.

## 3. Job sourcing and applications: do you apply for jobs on my behalf?

This is a key distinction. Career coaching, vacancy recommendations, recruiter introductions, and actually submitting applications are different services.

Ask:

- [ ] Do you actively search for suitable vacancies for me, or do you mainly teach me how to search?
- [ ] Will you send me curated job openings that match my profile, location preference (Munich / Germany), experience, and salary expectations? If so, how many and how often?
- [ ] Do you submit applications on my behalf, or am I responsible for every application?
- [ ] If you submit applications, who approves each role and application before submission?
- [ ] Do you contact recruiters or hiring managers directly, make referrals, or introduce me to partner companies? Please distinguish guaranteed deliverables from possibilities.
- [ ] Do you have established relationships with employers, or is the service based on public vacancies and general job-search guidance?
- [ ] Will you help me prioritize applications and tailor my CV for individual job descriptions?
- [ ] Can you show anonymized examples of the types of roles or companies that candidates with a similar background have pursued recently?
- [ ] How do you track applications, follow-ups, recruiter responses, and interview outcomes?
- [ ] Are there limits on the number of vacancies reviewed, applications supported, or recruiter introductions?

**Ask them to state clearly which of these are included:**

- [ ] Job-search strategy and target-company list
- [ ] Curated vacancy recommendations
- [ ] Application tracking
- [ ] Role-specific CV tailoring
- [ ] Application submission on my behalf
- [ ] Recruiter / hiring-manager outreach
- [ ] Referrals or direct employer introductions

Do not assume that “job-search support” means they will submit applications or arrange interviews.

## 4. Mock interviews and interview preparation

Ask:

- [ ] How many mock interviews are included in the package?
- [ ] How often can I book one—for example, weekly, every two weeks, or only when an interview is scheduled?
- [ ] How long is each mock interview?
- [ ] Will the mocks cover HR / behavioral questions, technical interviews, system design, and project deep dives, as relevant to the roles I target?
- [ ] Will the interviewer have experience recruiting or interviewing software engineers in Germany?
- [ ] Will I receive specific written feedback and an action plan after each session?
- [ ] Can we practice explaining my Manulife projects, frontend architecture, design-system work, Node.js experience, and trade-offs in English?
- [ ] If I receive a real interview invitation, can a mock interview be scheduled quickly enough to prepare for it?
- [ ] Are additional mock interviews included if I repeatedly fail at the same stage, or do they cost extra?

**Ask for measurable details:** number of mocks: ______; duration per mock: ______; feedback format: ______; rescheduling policy: ______.

## 5. Ongoing guidance if I cannot find a job

Do not settle for a general promise such as “we will support you until you succeed.” Ask what support actually happens when progress stalls.

- [ ] What happens if I do not receive recruiter responses after the first 20–30 targeted applications?
- [ ] What happens if I receive recruiter calls but fail to progress to technical interviews?
- [ ] What happens if I reach technical interviews but do not receive offers?
- [ ] How often will we review my job-search metrics and adjust the strategy?
- [ ] Will you analyze my CV conversion rate, recruiter response rate, interview pass rate, and feedback from rejected applications?
- [ ] Will you help identify whether the main issue is positioning, CV quality, English communication, technical interview performance, salary expectations, work authorization, or the target market?
- [ ] What specific retraining or practice will you recommend if I repeatedly fail at a particular stage?
- [ ] Is there a defined review or escalation process if progress remains limited for 4, 8, or 12 weeks?
- [ ] Does the service period have a fixed end date? Can it be extended, and at what cost?
- [ ] If I do not find a job before the service ends, what support—if any—continues afterward?
- [ ] Is there any refund, extension, or other remedy if the promised services are not delivered? Please distinguish this from a guarantee of employment.

**Ask them to describe a concrete example:** “If I have applied to 30 well-matched jobs over six weeks and have had no interviews, what exactly would your team do next, and when?”

## 6. Training and learning plan

Ask:

- [ ] Is there a structured training plan based on my current strengths and gaps, or do I receive general learning materials?
- [ ] What training is included for English-language behavioral interviews, technical interviews, and explaining projects?
- [ ] Do you provide exercises, sample answers, recordings, feedback, or homework between sessions?
- [ ] Will the training be tailored to German employers and my target roles?
- [ ] Do you help with salary negotiation, recruiter calls, explaining relocation motivation, and discussing work authorization?
- [ ] How will progress be measured? Will we agree on milestones and review them regularly?
- [ ] Are training materials and recordings available after the coaching session or after the contract ends?
- [ ] What additional courses or services would require separate payment?

## 7. Fee, success fee, and contract terms

The fee structure previously quoted to me was approximately **€800 upfront plus a one-time success fee of 12% of annual salary**. Confirm the current offer and every detail below in writing; do not rely on this estimate as the final contractual wording.

- [ ] What is the exact upfront fee, and does it include VAT or any other taxes?
- [ ] What exactly is the 12% calculated on: base annual salary, target total compensation, or another figure?
- [ ] Are bonuses, commissions, equity, signing bonuses, or benefits included in the calculation?
- [ ] When is the success fee triggered: when I receive an offer, sign an employment contract, start work, or receive my first salary?
- [ ] If I find a job independently without a referral or application from FAJIG, do I still owe the success fee?
- [ ] If I was already in a recruitment process before signing with FAJIG, how is that treated?
- [ ] If I receive more than one offer or change jobs during the contract period, when would a success fee apply?
- [ ] Is there a time limit after the service ends during which a job offer can still trigger the success fee?
- [ ] What happens if the employer withdraws the offer, the job is cancelled, or the employment ends during probation?
- [ ] Is the upfront fee refundable in any circumstances? What happens if FAJIG fails to deliver an agreed service?
- [ ] Can either party terminate the contract early? What notice period and charges apply?
- [ ] Are there automatic renewals, minimum terms, or additional fees?
- [ ] Which country's law governs the contract, and which court or dispute-resolution process applies?
- [ ] Can I review the full contract and take time to consider it before paying?

**Do not sign until the definitions of “success,” the fee calculation, independent job offers, termination, refunds, and post-contract obligations are clear.** If a clause is ambiguous or the potential liability is significant, consider having a German employment/contract lawyer review it.

## 8. Evidence of outcomes and independent references

Ask:

- [ ] Can you share recent, anonymized outcomes for clients with a profile similar to mine (experienced software engineers seeking work in Germany)?
- [ ] What do you mean by a successful outcome—interviews, offers, signed contracts, or actual starts?
- [ ] Over what period were those outcomes measured, and what proportion of all enrolled clients is included?
- [ ] How do you account for clients who pause the search, leave the program, or do not find a job?
- [ ] Can I speak with one or two former clients who have agreed to discuss their experience, including what worked and what did not?
- [ ] Can you provide an example of a support plan used when a candidate was not getting interviews?

Treat testimonials as useful context, not proof that the same outcome is likely for me. Ask for clear definitions and avoid relying only on selected success stories.

## 9. Communication, ownership, and practical arrangements

- [ ] Who is my day-to-day contact, and what is the backup if they are unavailable?
- [ ] What is the normal response time for messages and document reviews?
- [ ] Which time zone and working hours does the team use?
- [ ] Which tools will we use for calls, feedback, and tracking?
- [ ] Will I retain editable copies of all CVs, cover letters, trackers, and other materials created for me?
- [ ] How will my CV, contact details, and other personal information be stored, used, and shared with third parties?
- [ ] Will any application be submitted or any recruiter contacted using my personal information without my explicit approval?

## 10. Suggested closing questions for the consultation

Use these questions to move from broad promises to specific commitments:

1. “Could you summarize exactly what I will receive during the first 30 days?”
2. “What work will your team do for me, and what work must I do myself?”
3. “If I am not getting interviews after six weeks, what specific changes and support will you provide?”
4. “Could you send me the full service scope, schedule, fee calculation, and termination/refund terms in writing before I decide?”
5. “Which parts of the service are guaranteed deliverables, and which depend on market conditions or my own performance?”

### My decision rule

Proceed only if:

- The service scope and deliverables are specific enough to evaluate.
- I understand what FAJIG will do versus what I must do myself.
- The success-fee trigger and calculation are unambiguous.
- The contract explains termination, refunds, and obligations after the service ends.
- The expected value is credible for my own profile and budget.
- Any important verbal promise is confirmed in writing.

If answers remain vague, request clarification before signing. It is reasonable to take the contract away, review it carefully, and decide later.
