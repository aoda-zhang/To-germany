# Networking and referrals

## 12. 求职 Networking：联系公司员工并礼貌请求内推（英文模板）

> **使用原则：** 先建立真实、简短的联系，再视对方回应决定是否询问内推。不要一上来就要求对方替你背书；提供岗位链接、Job ID 和简历，降低对方的沟通成本。方括号内容需要按实际情况替换。LinkedIn 邀请备注通常有字数限制，优先使用模板 1。

### Template 1 — LinkedIn connection request（加好友时的简短备注）

**适用场景：** 第一次联系目标公司的员工，尚未建立对话。

**English**

Hi [Name], I’m a frontend-focused full-stack engineer with 9+ years of experience, mainly working with React and TypeScript. I came across your profile while learning more about [Company] and would be glad to connect.

**中文意思：** 你好，我是一名有 9 年以上经验、以前端为主的全栈工程师，主要使用 React 和 TypeScript。我在了解 [公司] 时看到了你的资料，希望能和你建立联系。

### Template 2 — Friendly greeting after they accept（对方通过后打招呼）

**适用场景：** 对方刚刚接受连接，先开启轻量对话，不急着要求内推。

**English**

Hi [Name], thanks for connecting! I’m exploring frontend-focused full-stack opportunities in Germany and noticed that you work at [Company]. I’d be interested to hear a little about your experience there whenever you have time.

**中文意思：** 你好，感谢通过连接！我正在关注德国的前端方向全栈岗位，注意到你在 [公司] 工作。如果你方便的话，我很希望了解一些你在那里的工作体验。

### Template 3 — Ask about a specific role and referral（针对具体岗位询问内推）

**适用场景：** 已有一定交流，或者对方明确表示愿意提供帮助。请附上真实岗位链接和 Job ID。

**English**

Hi [Name], I found this role at [Company] and it looks closely aligned with my experience in React, TypeScript, and frontend architecture: [Job Title / Job ID / Link]. If you feel my background could be a fit, would you be comfortable referring me? No worries at all if your company has a different referral process. I’m happy to share my CV and any other details that would help.

**中文意思：** 你好，我发现了 [公司] 的这个岗位，和我在 React、TypeScript 及前端架构方面的经验比较匹配。如果你认为我的背景合适，你是否方便帮我内推？如果公司有其他内推流程也完全没关系。我可以提供简历和其他所需信息。

### Template 4 — Ask for advice first（先请教，再视情况询问内推）

**适用场景：** 陌生联系人，暂时不确定是否适合直接提出内推请求。

**English**

Hi [Name], I’m considering applying for [Job Title] at [Company]. Since you have experience there, could I ask one quick question about the team’s focus or the skills that matter most for this role? I understand you may be busy, so even a brief pointer would be appreciated.

**中文意思：** 你好，我正在考虑申请 [公司] 的 [岗位名称]。你在公司有相关工作经验，我想请教一个简短的问题：这个团队主要关注什么，或者这个岗位最看重哪些技能？我知道你可能比较忙，即使给我一点简单建议，我也会很感激。

### Template 5 — Shared professional interest（从共同技术背景切入）

**适用场景：** 对方也从事前端、React、设计系统、微前端或相关技术；请只提及你确实观察到的共同点。

**English**

Hi [Name], I noticed your work around [React / design systems / microfrontends] at [Company]. I’ve worked on similar frontend challenges, including [brief, relevant example from your CV], so your experience caught my attention. I’d be happy to connect and exchange ideas.

**中文意思：** 你好，我注意到你在 [公司] 从事 [React / 设计系统 / 微前端] 相关工作。我也处理过类似的前端问题，例如 [简历中真实且相关的项目经历]，所以对你的经验很感兴趣。希望能建立联系、交流想法。

### Template 6 — Contact a recruiter or hiring team member（联系招聘人员）

**适用场景：** 对方是 recruiter、talent acquisition 或招聘团队成员，而不是普通员工内推。

**English**

Hi [Name], I’m interested in the [Job Title] position at [Company] ([Job ID or link]). I’m a frontend-focused full-stack engineer with 9+ years of experience, primarily in React and TypeScript, with additional Node.js experience. The role looks relevant to my background, and I’d appreciate any guidance on the application process or the team’s priorities. I can share my CV if useful.

**中文意思：** 你好，我对 [公司] 的 [岗位名称] 感兴趣（[岗位编号或链接]）。我是一名有 9 年以上经验、以前端为主的全栈工程师，主要使用 React 和 TypeScript，也有 Node.js 经验。这个岗位和我的背景比较相关，希望了解申请流程或团队最关注的能力。如有需要，我可以提供简历。

### Template 7 — Polite follow-up（礼貌跟进）

**适用场景：** 对方暂时没有回复。通常等待约 5–7 个工作日后再跟进一次即可，不要反复催促。

**English**

Hi [Name], I just wanted to follow up on my message below in case it got buried. I’m still interested in the [Job Title] role at [Company] ([link]). I completely understand if you’re busy or unable to help, and I appreciate your time either way.

**中文意思：** 你好，我想礼貌地跟进一下之前的消息，以防它被其他信息淹没。我仍然对 [公司] 的 [岗位名称] 感兴趣（[链接]）。我完全理解你可能比较忙，或者不方便提供帮助，无论如何都感谢你抽时间阅读。

### Template 8 — After someone agrees to refer you（对方同意内推后）

**适用场景：** 对方已经表示愿意内推，需要清晰提供材料。

**English**

Thanks a lot for offering to help, [Name] — I really appreciate it. Here are the details to make things easier:

- Role: [Job Title]
- Job ID / link: [Job ID / URL]
- CV: [attached / link]
- Short background: 9+ years in software engineering, frontend-focused full-stack development, with strong React and TypeScript experience and additional Node.js experience.

Please let me know if you need anything else from me. Thanks again!

**中文意思：** 非常感谢你愿意帮忙，我很感激。为了方便你操作，我把岗位信息、链接、简历和简短背景整理在这里。如果还需要其他材料，请告诉我。再次感谢！

### 使用建议

1. **第一次联系优先用 Template 1。** 邀请备注保持简短，不要在连接请求里塞入整份职业经历或长篇内推请求。
2. **有具体岗位时，提供岗位链接和 Job ID。** 对方才容易判断是否能内推，也能减少来回沟通。
3. **把请求变得容易拒绝。** 使用 “If you feel my background could be a fit” 和 “No worries if you’re unable to help”，不要让陌生人感到被施压。
4. **不要假装熟悉公司或对方经历。** 只有在看过其公开资料并确认属实时，才提及共同技术方向或共同背景。
5. **避免群发感。** 每条消息至少加入一个真实的个性化信息，例如岗位名称、对方负责的技术领域或你与岗位相关的项目经历。
6. **不要一开始就附加不必要的私人信息。** 首次沟通通常提供职业背景和岗位信息即可；只有对方需要时再发送完整简历。
7. **内推不是要求对方保证结果。** 你的目标是请对方帮忙提交申请或说明内部流程，而不是要求对方为尚未验证的能力作担保。

# Relocation preparation

This file tracks the practical preparation work alongside the job search. Confirm current requirements with the relevant German authorities before submitting documents, because requirements can vary by visa route and personal circumstances.

## Degree recognition

- [ ] Check whether your degree needs anabin verification and/or a ZAB Statement of Comparability for your intended route.
- [ ] Prepare your university diploma and degree certificate.
- [ ] Request an official university transcript, including any required stamp, signature, translation, or certification.
- [ ] Check whether any additional school-leaving or examination records are actually required for your specific application before spending time obtaining them.
- [ ] Keep digital copies of submitted documents and application references.

## Application materials

- [ ] Prepare a motivation letter if required by the route or application.
- [ ] Keep a record of German-language learning and any certificates.
- [ ] Keep the CV, LinkedIn profile, self-introduction, and interview answers consistent.
- [ ] Track each document's status, source, date obtained, and expiry date where applicable.

## Work authorization

- [ ] Confirm the correct work-authorization route for your situation and the latest EU Blue Card eligibility criteria.
- [ ] Be accurate in interviews about your current residence status, whether a visa is needed, and the earliest realistic start date.
- [ ] Do not claim that a Blue Card or residence permit has been approved until it has been granted.

## Language learning

- [ ] Keep German level descriptions consistent across the CV and interviews.
- [ ] Set a realistic next milestone and maintain regular speaking practice.
- [ ] Prepare a concise English explanation of how you are learning German and why it matters to your relocation.
